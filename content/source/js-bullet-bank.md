# Core Three (default for all roles)

- 1. Production — Redesigned production scheduling to sequence orders by due date, lead time, and available capacity, prioritizing long-lead wedding wear, achieving 98.6% on-time delivery

- 2. Supplier — Expanded the fabric supplier base from 4 to 12 vendors, sourcing on cost for Pret and on quality for Formal and Wedding, reducing fabric costs by 25% and rework to under 5% of order value

- 3. Cost Tracking — Implemented purchase-to-order cost tracking, linking material purchases to specific orders and SKUs and raising spend traceability to 93.5% for product-level margin control

Numbering is for reference only — do not carry the labels onto the resume.

Replaces: 1 Production — never combine with it, they share the same delivery evidence.

Use when: the posting emphasizes scaling, startup operations, hypergrowth, or working in ambiguity.

Swap C — E-commerce / D2C

Analyzed Instagram performance with the Digital & E-commerce Lead and adjusted content priorities, increasing 30-day unique reach by 36% and non-follower share from 24% to 31%

Replaces: 3 Cost Tracking.

Use when: the posting is genuinely D2C, e-commerce, or brand-marketing adjacent — not for general Business Analyst roles.

The Shopify funnel analysis (65% checkout drop-off, 1% conversion gate) is deliberately excluded. It ends in a threshold set rather than an improvement delivered, which breaks the rule that every J&S bullet closes on a measurable result. Keep it as an interview story.
