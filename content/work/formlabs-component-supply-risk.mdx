## Challenge

A single Formlabs printer draws on hundreds of components across mechanical, optical and electronic categories, with supplier lead times running from weeks to months.

The three things you need in order to see a shortage coming — inventory coverage, open purchase orders, delivery exceptions — sat in three separate places. Across 300+ components on two platforms, nobody reviews that by hand each week, so shortages tended to surface when a build could not start.

## What I did

1. **Pulled inventory coverage, open POs and delivery exceptions into one query layer** in SQL and BigQuery.
2. **Built shortage and exception reporting** across the 300+ SLA and SLS components, running against current data rather than a periodic manual extract.
3. **Reported risk as an exception**, so planner attention went to what had moved rather than to the full component list.
4. **Escalated flagged components to suppliers** while there was still lead time left to recover.

## Decision

> The reporting did not change a single supplier lead time. It moved the point at which a shortage became visible far enough forward that expediting, re-planning or escalating was still an option.

## Outcome

**Late orders down 20%**, with on-time in-full performance up 6%.
