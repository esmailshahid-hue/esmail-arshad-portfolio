## What I built

Freight Fulfillment Intelligence is a decision-support tool for freight marketplaces. It
shows which upcoming loads are at risk, what is driving the risk and what Operations should
do next. It combines demand, carrier capacity, historical acceptance and reliability, and
rate data to make those decisions.

A freight marketplace can have enough trucks available on paper and still struggle to cover
demand. Capacity only becomes useful when the right equipment is available on the right
lane, carriers are likely to accept the rate, service is reliable and the economics still
work.

The project uses synthetic MENA freight data and does not represent any company's actual
network or operations. It builds on my experience working with freight pricing, lane,
shipment and carrier data at Amazon.

## Core decisions

### Capacity coverage

Compares upcoming demand with qualified and effective carrier capacity to separate physical
shortages from capacity that looks available but is unlikely to convert.

### Pricing & economics

Tests whether higher carrier buy rates improve modeled acceptance and coverage without
breaching the commercial floor.

### Service & concentration

Shows when reliability or dependence on a small carrier group is reducing usable capacity.

### Operator actions

Prioritizes actions such as securing capacity, activating backup carriers, raising buy
rates, pre-booking supply or reviewing a commercial trade-off.

## How it works

Upcoming demand + carrier capacity + history → effective capacity → fulfillment risk → root
cause → recommended action → scenario testing

The tool uses four required datasets: upcoming loads, carrier capacity, historical loads and
historical carrier offers. Carrier payments are optional.

Users can inspect Capacity & Fulfillment, Pricing & Economics and Supply Gaps, then use
Scenario Lab to test changes against the same baseline decision engine.

## Build approach

- **Decision logic.** Calculations, risk classification, root-cause logic and recommended
  actions are defined in code rather than generated by a language model.
- **Scenario testing.** Baseline and changed assumptions use the same deterministic
  analytics engine, so the comparison stays consistent.
- **Local processing.** CSV parsing, validation, analytics and scenarios run in the browser.
  Raw uploaded files are not stored or sent to the AI provider.
- **Operations Brief.** DeepSeek can turn already-calculated results into a short summary
  when requested. It does not change calculations, priorities or recommendations.
