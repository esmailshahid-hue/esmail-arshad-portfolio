## Important: this supersedes an earlier recommendation
Do **not** use the previously suggested 4 + 3 experience-card grid. We are now deliberately replacing that idea with a **true horizontal career timeline on desktop**, inspired by the attached reference image.

Use the reference image only for the concept of:
- one continuous horizontal chronological axis
- role annotations that can alternate above and below the axis
- a clear visual relationship between each role and its place in time

However, **do not use evenly spaced role nodes**. The timeline must communicate not only sequence, but also **when each role occurred and approximately how long it lasted**.

Use a **semi-proportional career timeline / restrained Gantt hybrid**:

- each role starts at approximately the correct month within its year
- each role has a subtle horizontal duration span
- span length should approximately represent duration
- exact month ranges remain visible in text
- very short roles may use a sensible minimum display width so labels remain readable
- months are the source of truth; geometry is supporting visual information

Do **not** copy the reference image's rainbow colors, oversized dates, infographic styling, or dense paragraph blocks.

# 6. Experience: replace the current grid with a semi-proportional career timeline

## Why the timeline must encode duration
The current experience grid shows chronology but not duration. This is especially important because multiple roles occurred within the same calendar year, including Tesla and Formlabs in 2024.

Do **not** create seven evenly spaced role nodes. Equal spacing would incorrectly imply equal timing/duration.

The timeline must let a recruiter quickly understand:
1. where I worked
2. when I worked there
3. approximately how long each role lasted
4. how multiple roles within the same year relate to one another

## Desktop concept
Create one restrained **horizontal career timeline / Gantt hybrid** spanning the Experience section.

Use:
- a thin neutral horizontal time axis
- year markers from **2020 through 2026**
- subtle intermediate month / quarter positioning where needed
- role spans positioned according to their actual start and end months
- annotations alternating above and below the axis when useful for avoiding collisions
- small connectors from each annotation to its duration span
- one slightly emphasized current-role span
- lots of whitespace
- no miniature card borders
- no separate horizontal rule above every role

The final result should feel like a premium annual report or strategy-consulting timeline, not project-management software and not a colorful corporate infographic.

## Semi-proportional positioning rules
Use the dates below as the source of truth.

- Years should be evenly spaced across the primary axis.
- A role should begin at approximately the correct month within the relevant year.
- Its horizontal span should approximately represent the number of months worked.
- Do not force exact pixel-perfect mathematical proportionality if it makes short roles unreadable.
- Short internships may have a sensible minimum visual width.
- The exact month range printed beside the role is authoritative.
- If two roles touch or overlap in the same month, place them on separate lanes / opposite sides of the axis so the transition remains clear.
- Do not collapse Tesla and Formlabs into a generic `2024` grouping.
- Do not visually imply that a four-month internship lasted as long as a one-year or twenty-month role.
